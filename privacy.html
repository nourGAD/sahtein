Sahtein (صحتين) – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Sahtein is a diet and workout tracker developed by Nour Gad ("we"). This policy explains what information the app handles and how. The short version: everything you enter stays on your phone. We do not collect, receive, sell or share your personal data.

1. Information you enter

To calculate your daily calorie target and track your progress, the app stores the following only on your device:

  • Profile: sex, birth year, height, current and goal weight, activity level and weekly pace.
  • Meal times and reminder settings.
  • Foods and drinks you log, your weight entries, and workouts and sets you log.

This data is kept in a local database inside the app. It is not uploaded to our servers. We do not operate any servers for this app.

2. Voice input

If you use the microphone button, the app asks your phone's speech-recognition service (for example Google speech services) to convert your speech to text. The audio is processed by that service under its own privacy policy; Sahtein only receives the resulting text and does not record or store audio.

3. Exercise videos

The "Watch on YouTube" button opens YouTube outside the app. YouTube's own privacy policy applies to your use of YouTube.

4. Permissions

  • Notifications: to remind you at your meal times and send your daily summary.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarms): so reminders arrive on time.
  • Run at startup: to restore your reminders after the phone restarts.

The app does not request location, contacts, camera, storage or microphone permission.

5. Analytics, ads and third parties

The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking SDKs. We do not share data with third parties.

6. Backups

If Android backup is turned on in your phone settings, Android may include the app's data in your personal device backup (for example your Google account backup). This is controlled by you and your device provider, not by us.

7. Deleting your data

You can delete all your data at any time from Settings → Delete all my data, or by uninstalling the app.

8. Health information

Sahtein provides estimates for general wellness and is not a medical device or a substitute for professional medical advice. Consult a doctor before starting a diet, especially if you are under 18, pregnant, breastfeeding or have a medical condition.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes

If a future version adds features such as cloud backup or AI photo logging, we will update this policy before releasing them and explain what data is involved.

11. Contact

Questions: nour0gad@gmail.com

سياسة الخصوصية – صحتين

كل البيانات اللي بتدخلها في صحتين (بياناتك، الأكل، الوزن، التمارين) بتتخزن على موبايلك بس. إحنا مش بنجمع ولا بنستقبل ولا بنبيع ولا بنشارك أي بيانات شخصية، ومفيش سيرفرات للأبلكيشن.
